Traditions and lifestyle

The Culture of Rajasthan

A closer look at the food, clothes, art, music and festivals that make Rajasthan special.

🍛

Food

Dal Baati Churma: baked wheat baati, lentils and sweet churma.

Gatte ki Sabzi: gram-flour dumplings in spiced yoghurt gravy.

Ker Sangri: a traditional desert dish made with local beans and berries.

👗

Traditional Dress

Women's traditional clothing often includes ghagra, choli and odhni. Men's clothing can include dhoti or kurta with a safa (turban). Bandhani, leheriya and mirror-work fabrics are well known.

💃

Dance & Music

Ghoomar is a graceful folk dance. Kalbelia is known for energetic movements. Folk musicians use instruments such as kamayacha, sarangi, dholak and algoza.

🎉

Festivals

Gangaur, Teej, Pushkar Fair and the Desert Festival celebrate Rajasthan's traditions with music, fairs and colourful events.

🧵

Arts & Crafts

Rajasthan is known for blue pottery, block printing, miniature paintings, meenakari jewellery, leather mojari footwear and embroidery.

🏛️

Architecture

Forts, palaces, jharokhas, courtyards, stepwells and intricately carved havelis reflect the region's craftsmanship and history.

Clothing varies by region, community, weather and occasion. These are broad cultural examples, not a dress rule for every person.
About this project

Rajasthan Tourism Website

A detailed educational website made to introduce the history, destinations and cultural identity of Rajasthan.

Project Details

This website is created as a web development assignment using three basic technologies:

HTML

Used to create the structure, navigation, headings, city information and page content.

CSS

Used to design the colours, layout, cards, responsive sections and overall appearance.

JavaScript

Used to make city search interactive and display the selected city's detailed information.

Important note about historical dates

Some cities have a well-recorded foundation year, while ancient settlements may not have one exact known date. In those cases, this project uses wording such as “origins uncertain” or “traditional attribution” to avoid presenting uncertain history as fact.
